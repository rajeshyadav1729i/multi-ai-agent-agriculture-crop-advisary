# SCHEME REFERENCE: PRADHAN MANTRI KRISHI SINCHAYEE YOJANA (PMKSY) - PER DROP MORE CROP
MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE & FARMERS WELFARE / DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE & FARMERS WELFARE
Official Portal: https://pmksy.gov.in
Guidelines Ref: PDMC Component Guidelines

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE:
To enhance water use efficiency at the farm level through Micro Irrigation technologies, namely Drip and Sprinkler irrigation systems; to increase productivity of crops and income of farmer families; and to promote precision water application and fertigation.

2. FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE / SUBSIDIES:
- For Small & Marginal Farmers: 55% subsidy on indicative unit cost of drip/sprinkler system installation.
- For Other (Medium & Large) Farmers: 45% subsidy on indicative unit cost.
- Additional top-up subsidy provided by several State Governments (up to 70-90% total subsidy in specific states).
- Assistance available up to a maximum area limit of 5.0 hectares per beneficiary family.

3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- All farmers cultivating land with assured water source (borewell, tubewell, canal, farm pond, open well).
- Members of water user associations, cooperative societies, and self-help groups.
- Land ownership or registered long-term lease of minimum 7-10 years.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
- Aadhaar Card
- Proof of Land Ownership (7/12 extract, Jamabandi, or Sale deed)
- Electricity connection receipt / proof of water source availability
- Soil and Water test reports (if requested by district nodal agency)
- Bank Account Passbook copy
- Quotation / Estimate from empaneled micro-irrigation system manufacturers

5. APPLICATION PROCESS:
- Online application through the State Agriculture / Horticulture Department portal (e.g., e-Mausam / DBT Agriculture / State Micro Irrigation Portals).
- Physical inspection of the farm plot and water source by the Assistant Director of Horticulture/Agriculture.
- Work order issuance and direct subsidy credit to empaneled manufacturer or beneficiary DBT account after GPS-tagged post-installation verification.
