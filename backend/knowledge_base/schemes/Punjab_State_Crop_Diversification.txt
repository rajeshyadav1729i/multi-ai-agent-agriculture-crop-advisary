# SCHEME REFERENCE: PUNJAB STATE CROP DIVERSIFICATION & STUBBLE MANAGEMENT SCHEME
DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE AND FARMERS WELFARE, GOVERNMENT OF PUNJAB
Official Portal: https://agri.punjab.gov.in
Notification Ref: Pb-Agri-Diversification-2024/782

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE:
To encourage farmers in Punjab to adopt sustainable crop rotations, conserve depleting groundwater aquifers, diversify from water-guzzling crops, adopt high-yield certified seeds for Wheat, Maize, Pulses, and Oilseeds, and incentivize residue management through in-situ machinery.

2. FINANCIAL INCENTIVES:
- Direct financial incentive of Rs. 7,000 per acre for farmers diversifying from water-intensive crops to alternative crops (Maize, Pulses, Cotton).
- Subsidies up to 50% for individual farmers and 80% for Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) on modern agricultural implements (Super SMS, Happy Seeder, Smart Seeder, Mulcher, Rotavator, Laser Land Leveller).
- Distribution of certified Wheat and Rabi crop seeds at 50% subsidized rates up to Rs. 1,000 per quintal directly via DBT to farmer accounts.

3. ELIGIBILITY:
- Landholding farmers in Punjab registered on the 'Anaaj Kharid' portal and Department of Agriculture portal.
- Farmers practicing crop diversification or using in-situ stubble management machines.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
- Aadhaar Card
- Land ownership (Fard / Jamabandi)
- Anaaj Kharid Farmer Registration ID
- Bank Passbook (Aadhaar linked)

5. APPLICATION:
- Apply online through the Punjab Agriculture Department DBT portal (agrimachinerypb.com / agri.punjab.gov.in).
